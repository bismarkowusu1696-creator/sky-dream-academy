About SkyDream

SkyDream Skills Training Academy provides practical, hands-on training intended to help learners build useful skills for work, self-employment and further development.

Our approach

Learn by doing

Our training combines instruction, guided practice and real tasks. The academy currently offers 13 programs spanning technical skills, creative trades, beauty, food, languages and business skills.

We are based in Accra, Ghana. For directions to the training centre, contact the academy using the office or WhatsApp numbers shown on the Contact page.
