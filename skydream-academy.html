About Us

Practical training, real opportunity

Our History

SkyDream Skills Training Academy was founded to give school leavers, career changers and entrepreneurs in Accra a practical route into skilled work — training that fits around a job, on Saturdays and Sundays, without long-term commitment or heavy fees.

Our Vision

A community where every young person within reach of SkyDream has a marketable skill and the confidence to earn from it.

Our Mission

To deliver hands-on, affordable, weekend vocational training across trades, languages and digital skills — and to support every graduate into work or self-employment.

Our Objectives

  • Equip 500+ learners yearly across 13 vocational tracks
  • Keep training affordable and weekend-accessible
  • Partner with local businesses for apprenticeships and job placement
  • Maintain small class sizes for hands-on mentorship
