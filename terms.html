Website use

This website provides information about SkyDream Skills Training Academy, its training programs, schedules and registration services. Users should provide accurate information and must not attempt to gain unauthorised access to academy systems or student records.

Registration

Submitting an online registration creates a student-registration record or, where a program has reached capacity, may add the applicant to a waitlist. A successful submission does not override academy eligibility, capacity, payment or administrative requirements.

Program schedules and availability

Published dates and program information reflect the academy's current plans. The academy may make reasonable changes to schedules, facilitators, capacity or training arrangements when necessary. Registered students should follow official academy communications for updates.

Fees and payments

The website currently displays a GHS 50 registration fee for the current intake. Any additional tuition, materials or program-related fees should be confirmed with the academy before payment. Payment records shown in the status tool reflect amounts entered in the academy's system.

Student status lookup

The status tool is intended for the registered student. Users must provide the matching registration number and mobile number. Attempting to access another person's record without permission is prohibited.

Program descriptions are provided for general information. Specific lesson content, equipment and practical activities may vary by intake and facilitator.
